# Refund Policy

**Effective:** 24 September 2026  
**Last updated:** 24 September 2026

This policy applies to purchases made directly from How To Use AI.com where **RePass Cloud Pty Ltd** is the seller.

## Consumer rights

Nothing in this policy excludes or limits a right or remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded, including Australian Consumer Law consumer guarantees where they apply.

No blanket "no refunds" rule applies.

## Digital books

If a purchased ebook is missing, corrupt, inaccessible because the supplied file itself is faulty, or the wrong file is supplied, contact hello@repasscloud.com.

We will normally first attempt to provide or re-provide the correct functioning file promptly.

If the issue cannot be remedied within a reasonable time, or the failure is major, the customer receives the remedy required by applicable law.

## Printed books

If a directly purchased printed book is faulty, damaged, materially misdescribed, incorrect or not delivered as required, contact support with the order number and reasonable evidence such as photographs or tracking information.

We may work with the printer, fulfilment provider or carrier to assess and resolve the issue.

Applicable remedies may include replacement, refund or another remedy required by law.

## Major and minor failures

Where applicable law treats a failure as major and gives the customer a statutory choice of remedy, that choice will be respected.

Where a failure is minor and can lawfully be remedied, we may first provide an appropriate remedy within a reasonable time.

If the problem is not remedied within a reasonable time, further statutory remedies may apply.

## Change of mind

No general voluntary change-of-mind refund or return is promised at launch.

This does not affect a statutory remedy.

A future product or promotion may offer additional voluntary return rights. Those additional terms will be clearly stated with the offer.

## Orders not supplied

If an order is not supplied within a specifically promised time or, where no time was promised, within a reasonable time, we will investigate and provide the remedy required by applicable law.

## Seller cancellation

If RePass Cloud cancels an accepted paid order before supply because of a genuine and reasonably justified reason such as fraud, an obvious material pricing error, legal inability to supply or genuine unavailability, the amount paid for the cancelled item and any directly associated refundable charge will be returned.

## Timing

We aim to:

- acknowledge a request within 5 business days;
- provide an outcome or meaningful progress update within 10 business days where reasonably practicable; and
- submit an approved refund promptly, normally within 5 business days after approval.

Refunds are normally sent to the original payment method.

Banks and payment providers may take additional time to make funds visible after a refund is submitted.

## Retailer purchases

If you bought the book through Amazon, Apple Books or another retailer, use that retailer's refund and order-support process in the first instance because it operates the checkout and payment.

RePass Cloud can assist with publisher information, source-file defects, corrections, accessibility, rights questions and finding the appropriate support route.
