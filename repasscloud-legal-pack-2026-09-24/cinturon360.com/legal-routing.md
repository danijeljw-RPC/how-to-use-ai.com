# Cinturon360 Legal Routing

## Public marketing website

The public marketing website should link to:

- `https://repasscloud.com/legal/privacy-policy/`
- `https://repasscloud.com/legal/terms-of-service/`
- `https://repasscloud.com/legal/refund-policy/`
- the Cinturon360 marketing-site notices in this package where a local notice is desired.

## Actual Cinturon360 product

Continue to use the existing product-specific documents for the application and enterprise customers.

Those may include:

- product Privacy Policy or Notice;
- application Terms of Service;
- Refund or Cancellation Policy;
- Data Processing Agreement;
- subprocessors/data residency information;
- security documentation;
- enterprise order forms and customer agreements; and
- service-level/support terms.

The master RePass Cloud policies should operate as the corporate baseline, not as a replacement for negotiated or product-specific enterprise commitments.
