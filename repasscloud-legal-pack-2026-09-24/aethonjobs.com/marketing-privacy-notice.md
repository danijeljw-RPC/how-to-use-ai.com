## Public marketing website

When you browse the public marketing site, RePass Cloud and its infrastructure providers may process ordinary technical request data needed to deliver, secure and maintain the site.

If you submit a public contact, waitlist or similar form, we may process the information you provide to respond, administer the request, prevent abuse and maintain appropriate business records.

## Aethon Jobs application

The Aethon Jobs application handles information and workflows that go beyond ordinary marketing-site use, which may include:

- candidate accounts and profiles;
- resumes and employment or education information;
- job applications;
- employer and recruiter accounts;
- organisation verification;
- job listings;
- payment or credit information;
- account-security and audit information; and
- AI-assisted matching, ranking or application-analysis features.

The application must provide or link to its own product-specific privacy documentation describing those activities in appropriate detail.

Where the application-specific privacy notice differs from this marketing-site notice or the RePass Cloud master policy, the application-specific notice governs the application processing to which it expressly applies.

## Tracking and advertising

Aethon public material currently represents the product as avoiding tracking, analytics cookies and data brokerage and using only necessary session or security technologies for the application.

Those public statements should be kept aligned with the actual deployed marketing site and application. If analytics, advertising or non-essential tracking is introduced, the applicable notice and user controls must be updated before or when the change is made.
