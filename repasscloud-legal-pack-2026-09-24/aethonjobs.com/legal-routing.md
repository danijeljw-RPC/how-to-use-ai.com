# Aethon Jobs Legal Routing

## Public marketing site

Link to:

- RePass Cloud Privacy Policy
- RePass Cloud Terms of Service
- RePass Cloud Refund Policy
- Aethon Jobs Marketing Site Privacy Notice
- Aethon Jobs Marketing Site Terms

## Application

The application must separately link to its product-specific:

- Privacy Notice
- Platform Terms
- Refund/payment terms where users pay for verification, credits or listings
- Any employer/recruiter terms
- Any AI/matching explanation required for the actual workflow
- Any data-processing terms used for organisation customers

Do not replace those application documents with the short marketing-site notices.

## Operator-name check

Public Aethon pages currently refer to "Aethon Software" in some places.

Before changing the application legal documents, confirm whether "Aethon Software" is:

- only a product/brand/business name of RePass Cloud Pty Ltd; or
- a separate legal entity.

The legal contracting entity and privacy controller must be named consistently throughout checkout, invoices, privacy notices, platform terms and the footer.
