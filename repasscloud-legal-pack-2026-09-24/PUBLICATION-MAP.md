# Publication Map

## RePass Cloud parent site

| Route | File | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/legal/privacy-policy/` | `repasscloud.com/privacy-policy.md` | Master privacy policy |
| `/legal/terms-of-service/` | `repasscloud.com/terms-of-service.md` | Master terms |
| `/legal/refund-policy/` | `repasscloud.com/refund-policy.md` | Master refunds/remedies |

All RePass Cloud websites, apps and tools should link to these master documents unless a product-specific legal page applies.

## How To Use AI.com

| Route | File | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/privacy/` | `how-to-use-ai.com/privacy-policy.md` | Site-specific privacy notice supplementing the master Privacy Policy |
| `/terms/` | `how-to-use-ai.com/terms-of-service.md` | Site-specific terms supplementing the master Terms |
| Local refund page if desired | `how-to-use-ai.com/refund-policy.md` | Book/digital/print refund supplement |

The footer should also link to the three RePass Cloud master policies.

## Aethon Jobs

Use the marketing-site files for `aethonjobs.com`.

The actual Aethon Jobs application should continue to display product-specific privacy and platform terms because candidate, resume, employer, recruiter, application, verification, payment and AI-assisted matching data require product-specific disclosures.

## Cinturon360

Use the marketing-site files for the public `cinturon360.com` marketing pages.

Do not replace Cinturon360 application/customer legal documents with the short marketing-site notices. The product-specific SaaS privacy, service terms, data-processing terms, commercial terms and refund/cancellation provisions remain the more specific documents.

## Small tools

For a small RePass Cloud utility with no special data handling:

- link directly to the RePass Cloud master policies; and
- optionally publish the reusable notices in `reusable-tools/`.

If a tool has a repository licence such as MIT, Apache-2.0 or GPL, that software licence governs the source-code rights and is additional to the website Terms.
