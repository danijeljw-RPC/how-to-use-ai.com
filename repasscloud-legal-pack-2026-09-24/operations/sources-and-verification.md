# Sources and Verification Notes

This file records the main source material and public references used to prepare and check the policy architecture.

## Authoritative factual brief

The primary source was the completed **Privacy Policy and Website Terms Questionnaire** supplied on 24 September 2026.

It provides the operator identity, legal identifiers, public address, contact channels, website scope, data inventory, Cloudflare/D1/Turnstile configuration, retention schedule, planned Stripe checkout, mailing-list plan, direct-sales model, refund approach and operational-readiness information used in this package.

Where the questionnaire left a provider or future feature undecided, the public policies use conditional wording instead of inventing a provider or pretending the feature is live.

## RePass Cloud public legal pages reviewed

- Terms of Service:  
  https://repasscloud.com/legal/terms-of-service/

- Privacy Policy:  
  https://repasscloud.com/legal/privacy-policy/

- Refund Policy:

The replacement pack follows the later questionnaire where it conflicts with older parent-site wording. In particular, the package uses South Australia for the new master governing-law clause, uses the supplied Findon address, and does not carry forward a general public liability cap or general user indemnity.

## Australian privacy

OAIC — Small business:  
https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-guidance-for-organisations-and-government-agencies/organisations/small-business

OAIC — Opting in to the Privacy Act:  
https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-guidance-for-organisations-and-government-agencies/organisations/opting-in-to-the-privacy-act

OAIC — Australian Privacy Principles Guidelines:  
https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines

Operational point: the Privacy Act's small-business rules and exceptions need to be assessed against the business's actual activities. The master policy therefore uses the Australian Privacy Principles as a privacy baseline without asserting formal APP-entity status for every activity.

## Australian marketing and spam

ACMA — Avoid sending spam:  
https://www.acma.gov.au/avoid-sending-spam

Implementation points reflected in the checklist include a clear unsubscribe mechanism, timely action on requests, continued availability of the unsubscribe facility and no requirement to log in or provide unnecessary extra information to unsubscribe.
