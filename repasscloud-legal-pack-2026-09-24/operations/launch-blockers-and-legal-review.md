# Launch Blockers and Legal Review Items

These are not placeholders for the public policies. The public documents have been drafted so that unresolved features are not falsely represented as live.

## 1. GST and tax treatment

Before How To Use AI.com direct checkout is enabled, confirm:

- whether RePass Cloud Pty Ltd is currently registered or required to be registered for GST;
- whether displayed Australian prices must include GST;
- receipt and tax-invoice wording;
- treatment of overseas digital sales and physical-book sales; and
- whether any overseas VAT, GST or sales-tax registration or marketplace rules apply.

## 2. Mailing provider

A mailing provider has not yet been selected.

Before transferring subscriber data:

- identify the legal entity;
- review privacy terms and DPA;
- record storage and processing countries;
- review subprocessors;
- confirm deletion, export and suppression controls;
- implement double opt-in and unsubscribe; and
- update the site policy if the provider creates material new processing.

## 3. Direct print fulfilment

Do not enable direct print-book checkout until the printer, print-on-demand or fulfilment provider is selected and shipping, returns, privacy and processing locations are documented.

## 4. Email, accounting and backup providers

The questionnaire did not identify all business email, accounting/bookkeeping or separate backup providers.

The master Privacy Policy therefore describes provider categories rather than inventing names.

Internally document any provider that handles personal information.

## 5. EEA targeting

The questionnaire states an intention to deliberately target Spain, Italy, Croatia and Germany.

EU GDPR territorial scope can reach an organisation outside the EU where processing relates to offering goods or services to people in the EU or monitoring their behaviour there.

Before deliberate EEA marketing or direct sales at scale, obtain advice on:

- Article 3 territorial scope for the actual activity;
- lawful-basis mapping;
- Article 27 representative requirements and any exception;
- international transfer mechanisms;
- cookie and ePrivacy requirements;
- consumer/ecommerce information requirements;
- data-subject rights workflow; and
- whether an EU-facing privacy supplement is required.

If a representative is required, their details need to be made available to affected people.

## 6. United Kingdom targeting

The questionnaire states an intention to deliberately target the United Kingdom.

UK GDPR can apply to an organisation outside the UK that offers goods or services to people in the UK or monitors behaviour there.

Before deliberate UK targeting at scale, review:

- UK GDPR territorial scope;
- lawful bases;
- UK representative requirements and exceptions;
- international transfers;
- PECR, cookie and direct-marketing rules; and
- consumer/ecommerce obligations.

## 7. Australian Privacy Act position

The questionnaire gives annual turnover as AUD 0–100,000 and does not identify an obvious Privacy Act small-business exception merely from keeping ordinary sales/CRM information.

Most small businesses with annual turnover of AUD 3 million or less are not covered by the Privacy Act unless an exception applies. A qualifying small business can also formally opt in to Privacy Act coverage.

The master Privacy Policy deliberately adopts the Australian Privacy Principles as a privacy baseline without claiming that RePass Cloud has formally opted in or is necessarily an APP entity for every activity.

If RePass Cloud wants to describe itself as an APP entity regardless of turnover, confirm the legal basis or complete the formal opt-in process before making that claim.

## 8. Aethon Jobs operator identity

Public Aethon material uses the name "Aethon Software" in some places.

Confirm whether Aethon Software is:

- a separate incorporated legal entity; or
- a brand, business name or product operated by RePass Cloud Pty Ltd.

Do not publish contradictory controller, seller or contracting-party identities.

## 9. Aethon application privacy

Do not replace Aethon's detailed product privacy notice with the generic RePass Cloud parent policy.

Aethon's public material describes candidate profiles, resumes, applications, employer or recruiter workflows, verification and AI-assisted matching. Those matters need application-specific disclosure and operational verification.

## 10. Cinturon360 application terms

The parent policy architecture should not displace signed enterprise/customer agreements, DPAs, data-residency commitments, security terms, product-specific refund/cancellation rules or other Cinturon360 application documentation.

## 11. Liability and indemnity

The questionnaire explicitly states:

- no general monetary liability cap has been approved;
- no general user indemnity is required; and
- bespoke liability exclusions should receive Australian legal review.

The replacement master Terms therefore do not reproduce a general public liability cap or general user indemnity.

## 12. Legal review

Before materially expanding paid sales or international targeting, have an Australian lawyer review:

- the master parent Terms;
- the master Refund Policy;
- the How To Use AI.com direct-sale terms;
- GST and consumer pricing;
- international privacy and ecommerce scope;
- any proposed liability allocation; and
- product-specific terms for Aethon Jobs and Cinturon360.

This file is an operational review checklist and is not intended for publication as a customer-facing policy.
