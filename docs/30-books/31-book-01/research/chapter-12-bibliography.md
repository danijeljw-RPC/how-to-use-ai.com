## Evidence Decisions

- **"More important" defined.** The Key Idea is kept but qualified; the introduction separates four meanings (economic complement, larger share of remaining work, safety/accountability, personal/social importance), following package file 01 §1 and template observation 1.
- **Durable ≠ uniquely human.** Defined by what a capability lets a person do (direct, recognise bad answers, add context, choose, take responsibility, recover, function without the tool), following package 00 and 03. AI performance on empathy, persuasion, theory-of-mind and ideation tasks is stated early, with limits.
- **Effort vs judgement as a moving line,** not a partition (package 01 §11; observation 2). Chapter 6's "friction you don't value vs practice you do" framing and Chapter 8/10's spectrum are referenced rather than re-explained.
- **Six vs seven.** Adaptability is named as the throughline in "Seven Capabilities, Six Sections" (package 01 §9; observation 16). Domain expertise and verification are named as cross-cutting foundations rather than new sections.
- **Evidence types preserved.** Deming is labelled historical and pre-GenAI; WEF as employer expectations; OECD as developing evidence; JSA as a framing; bounded AI tests are described by task and setting; deskilling studies carry their design limits.
- **Continuity, not repetition.** Customer-support, jagged-frontier and PNAS tutoring studies are recalled briefly with pointers to Chapters 7, 10 and 11 and use the same figures as those chapters (published QJE figures: 5,172 agents, ~15 per cent). Creativity practices point back to Chapter 8. The 2026 "choose human empathy" study is reused from Chapter 4's footnote.
- **Callouts.** Per ADR-04-0002: Key Idea, Watch Out, Try This, Recap. The plan's Example callout became the in-prose "Judgement in Action" worked example; the Reflection callout became closing questions in the Recap.
- **Tables, no diagram.** A "Six Skills at a Glance" table (package 08 observation 20; 09 §2) and a supervision-paradox table (package 04 §10) were added. No Mermaid diagram: the plan requires none, and the tables carry the comparison better than a flow diagram.
- **Generic teaching scenarios** (family appointments, football club, school pick-up, second car, missed birthday, family care decision) are labelled in Chapter Notes as illustrations; no author experience invented.
- **Not used in prose:** UNESCO frameworks and the EU AI-literacy obligation (Chapter 13 boundary); Autor 2024 and Deming & Kahn (no added reader value beyond Deming 2017); Microsoft "Shifting Work Patterns" (time-use, not skill); Review of Managerial Science AI leadership review (too general); Systems Thinking Scale (measurement detail); Law Council resources.

## Corrections and Refinements to the Research Package

- **Dayal matter.** Checked against the regulator statement on 1 October 2026: the Board's decision was dated **19 August 2025** (statement published 2 September 2025); the authorities were tendered to the **Federal Circuit and Family Court**; conditions included no practice as a principal, no trust money, employed solicitor only, and two years' supervised practice with quarterly reporting. The package gave only the statement date.
- **Customer-support figures.** The package used working-paper figures (5,179 agents, ~14 per cent). The manuscript follows Chapters 7 and 10 in using the published version (5,172 agents, ~15 per cent).
- **Federal Court practice note.** The 1 October retrieval failed. The official note was retrieved and read on 4 October 2026; §§4.3–4.6 support the chapter’s verification duty.
- **Author names.** Publisher pages and publisher-deposited Crossref records checked on 4 October supplied authors and exact titles for persuasion, disclosure, apology, emotional-competency training, leadership and systems reviews. The endnotes now identify them. Metadata checks do not imply full-text access.
