# How This Book Works

Most of this book is ordinary reading: plain-English explanations, everyday examples, and the occasional table. Every so often, though, you'll come across a shaded or outlined box with a small coloured label in its top corner. Those boxes pull out the handful of things worth stopping for.

There are five kinds, and each one always means the same thing.

> **Key Idea:** The one thing to remember from a section. If you only skim a chapter, read these.

> **Try This:** A small, practical thing you can do right now, usually in a few minutes and without any technical setup. You don't have to do them all, but they're the quickest way to turn reading into experience.

> **Watch Out:** A risk, a limitation, or a common misunderstanding. These are the places where AI tools most often catch people out, so they're worth slowing down for.

> **Recap:** A short summary at the end of each chapter, often with a question to think about. Use it to check that the chapter landed before you move on.

You can read the chapters in order, which is how the ideas build, or dip into the chapter that matches what you need today. Either way, the boxes are there to help you find your footing quickly.
