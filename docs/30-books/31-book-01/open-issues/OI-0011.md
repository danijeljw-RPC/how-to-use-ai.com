## Area

## Question

Can the three remaining blocked URLs be confirmed in an ordinary browser, and can the invalid ACCC report URL be corrected in the manuscript before the next publication build?

## Evidence and Required Actions

The [manual URL report](../research/url-manual-check-2026-10-05.md) records all 46 checks and their methods.

- Chapter 9, `ch9-scam-figures`: original Targeting Scams Report 2025 URL displays a missing-page response. The report records a browser-checked official replacement. Apply that path to the chapter citation and rebuild/check the relevant publication outputs. This task records the correction in research only.
- Chapter 8, `ch8-us-cases`: Reuters original remains device-verification blocked. A browser-checked WHBL copy of the Reuters dispatch is recorded as an alternative. Confirm the original in an ordinary browser or use the checked syndicated copy with appropriate attribution.
- Chapter 12, `ch12-disclosure`: ScienceDirect corporate-apology paper remains CAPTCHA/403 blocked. Its exact publisher URL is indexed with the expected title and DOI, but live access is unverified.
- Chapter 14, `ch14-detectors`: ScienceDirect detector paper remains CAPTCHA/403 blocked. Its exact publisher URL is indexed with the expected title and DOI, but live access is unverified.
- Reconcile the earlier full-inventory summary: its categories sum to 470 against a stated 483 unique URLs. The current pass examines only the 46 listed URLs and cannot account for the other 13.

## Current Assumptions

Security challenges and search-index records do not establish that a URL is dead. No archive replacement is approved unless its content is checked, as required by ADR-03-0011. The ACCC source exists on its official site and only its cited path needs correction.

## Resolution

Resolved 5 October 2026.

- Chapter 9, `ch9-scam-figures`: the checked official ACCC replacement URL now replaces the missing-page path in the chapter, `research/chapter-09-bibliography.md` and the Chapter 9 research package (`03-scams-and-fraud.md`, `13-source-catalogue.md`). The release was rebuilt and rechecked.
- Chapter 8 `ch8-us-cases` (Reuters), Chapter 12 `ch12-disclosure` and Chapter 14 `ch14-detectors` (ScienceDirect): the author opened each original URL in an ordinary browser and confirmed the expected page. The original URLs stay; the WHBL copy is not needed.
- Inventory count: the earlier table left out 11 successful HTTP 203/202 responses and two URLs confirmed on retry. Corrected, its categories sum to 483. Recorded in the [manual URL report](../research/url-manual-check-2026-10-05.md).

All 46 checklist URLs are now confirmed or corrected.
