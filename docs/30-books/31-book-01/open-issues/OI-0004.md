## Priority 1 — Needs Confirmation Before It Can Stand

These claims are in the manuscript but are **not** fully supported by the two research packages. They came from general knowledge or had a citation problem.

### 1. The 2025 trial ruling in the Anthropic case

- [ ] Resolved

- **Where:** "Why the Answer Changes at the Border", United States paragraph (about line 313).
- **Text:** "the trial judge ruled in 2025 that training on lawfully acquired books was fair use, but that building a library from pirated copies was a separate matter"
- **Why flagged:** The research packages only cover the US$1.5 billion settlement and its final approval, from an Authors Guild summary. The description of the earlier ruling came from general knowledge of the June 2025 order in *Bartz v. Anthropic*.
- **What to check:** The court's summary-judgment order (N.D. Cal., June 2025), and that the settlement figure and final-approval date match the court record, not only the Authors Guild summary.
- **If it can't be confirmed:** Delete the clause about the trial ruling and keep only the settlement sentence.

### 2. Australia's lack of a general "right of publicity"

- [ ] Resolved

- **Where:** "Music and Audio", voice-cloning paragraph (about line 125).
- **Text:** "Australia, for instance, has no single general 'right of publicity' of the kind recognised in some US states; complaints about the unauthorised use of someone's identity tend to be argued through other routes, such as passing off or misleading-conduct law."
- **Why flagged:** This is standard Australian legal background, but it has no source in either research package and no footnote.
- **What to check:** Find a citable source, such as an Arts Law Centre information sheet on personality or publicity rights, or ACCC guidance on misleading endorsements. Add it to the `ch8-replicas` footnote and the bibliography.
- **If it can't be sourced:** Soften it to "Rules protecting a person's identity differ from country to country" and drop the Australia-specific detail.

### 3. Author names for the image-bias study

- [ ] Resolved

- **Where:** Footnote `ch8-bias` (about line 534).
- **Text:** "AlDahoul, Rahwan and Zaki"
- **Why flagged:** Earlier drafts gave the authors' given names inconsistently ("Zubair Rahwan" in the bibliography). Surnames only are used until confirmed.
- **What to check:** The *Scientific Reports* article page (<https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-99623-3>). Correct both the footnote and the bibliography.

### 4. Kandpal et al. citation (already corrected, for your awareness)

- [x] Resolved on 2026-09-30

- **Where:** Footnote `ch8-memorisation`.
- **What happened:** Both research packages, and the previous draft, linked the paper "Deduplicating Training Data Mitigates Privacy Risks in Language Models" to an ACL Anthology page that belongs to a *different* paper (Lee et al.). The citation now points to the correct ICML 2022 record (<https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/kandpal22a.html>), which was fetched and confirmed.
- **Action for you:** None required. Consider correcting source S28 in the extended package's source catalogue so the error doesn't spread to other chapters.

## Priority 2 — Time-Sensitive Facts to Recheck Near Publication

These claims come from the research packages and were accurate as of 30 September 2026. They were **not** independently re-browsed during the expansion, and they are the kind of fact that changes. Recheck each one shortly before publication.

### Law and policy

- [ ] **Australia:** the government is "not considering a text-and-data-mining exception" and CAIRG's priorities (about lines 306–311). Source: Attorney-General's Department CAIRG page.
- [ ] **United States:** *Thomson Reuters v. ROSS* was upheld on appeal, with the reasoning sealed at the time of writing (about line 313). Check whether the opinion has been published and whether its reasoning changes how the chapter describes it.
- [ ] **United States:** the Copyright Office Part 3 report is still a "pre-publication version". Check for a final version.
- [ ] **United States:** check for any newer major AI-training decisions that would make the two-case illustration look out of date.
- [ ] **European Union:** AI Act obligations for general-purpose models, and the training-content summary template.
- [ ] **United Kingdom:** the broad opt-out exception is "no longer its preferred" approach, and the proposal to remove computer-generated-works protection (about line 317). Check whether either has been legislated or changed.
- [ ] **SAG-AFTRA:** the digital-replica consent requirements (about line 125).

### Provider terms and product features

- [ ] **Suno:** free plan is non-commercial; paid plans get commercial rights and ownership "as between" user and Suno; no guarantee of copyright (about line 123, and the ownership table).
- [ ] **Canva:** AI Product Terms on ownership "as between" the parties, and outputs possibly not being unique (ownership table, about line 337).
- [ ] **Adobe Firefly:** licensed and public-domain training, contributor bonuses, no individual Stock opt-out.
- [ ] **Adobe:** Content Credentials automatically applied to fully Firefly-generated images (about line 353).
- [ ] **OpenAI:** C2PA metadata plus invisible watermarking on image outputs (about line 353).
- [ ] **Adobe Premiere Generative Extend** (about line 151) and **Canva Magic Design** (about line 163): check both features still exist and work as described.

### Platform policies

- [ ] **Amazon KDP:** requires disclosure of AI-generated content but not AI-assisted content (about line 81).
- [ ] **YouTube:** disclosure of realistic altered or synthetic content, including its examples of cloned voices and synthetic music (about line 159).

### Industry figures and deals

- [ ] **Deezer:** the upload trend (about 10% → 44% → over 50%, about 90,000 a day), the 1–3% listening share, and fraud demonetisation (about line 129). **If these figures change, also update the diagram `creative-abundance-bottleneck.mmd`, which repeats them.**
- [ ] **Spotify:** more than 75 million "spammy" tracks removed (about line 133).
- [ ] **UMG–Udio and WMG–Suno:** check whether the licensed platforms and artist opt-in controls actually launched as announced (about line 127).
- [ ] **Anthropic settlement:** status and figure, checked against the court record (see Priority 1, item 1).
