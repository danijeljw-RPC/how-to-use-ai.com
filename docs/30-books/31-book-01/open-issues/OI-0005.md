## Resolution

All eight claims were checked against primary or authoritative sources on 2026-10-04:

1. **Extinction-risk statement:** the wording matches safe.ai exactly.
2. **Protein structure:** a 50-year-old problem, AlphaFold2 in 2020, about 200 million proteins, and the 2024 Chemistry Nobel shared with Baker's protein-design work all confirmed (Nobel announcement coverage).
3. **Weather model:** "under one minute" for 10-day forecasts, and better than HRES on 90% of 1,380 verification targets (Lam et al., *Science* 2023, abstract).
4. **Expert survey:** the abstract says the 2047 estimate is 13 years earlier than the 2022 survey's (2060).
5. **Bar exam:** Martínez confirms the 90th percentile was based on a February administration skewed towards repeat takers; 68th / 63rd / 48th (15th on essays) confirmed.
6. **Headlines and press releases:** about 105,000 headline variations (Robertson et al. 2023); exaggerated press releases associated with much more exaggerated news (odds ratios 6.5, 20 and 56; Sumner et al. 2014).
7. **Chess:** the cited National Academies source dates the Newell and Simon prediction to **1957**, not 1958. Corrected in Chapter 11, its footnote, the Chapter 11 and 14 bibliographies, and Chapter 14's cross-reference. Deep Blue's 1997 match win confirmed.
8. **Builder.ai:** The Pragmatic Engineer, "The Pulse #137" (12 June 2025), corrected the viral claim after speaking to former engineers. Date added to the footnote.

Footnotes completed: `ch11-anthro` (van Es and Nguyen, *AI & Society* 40(5), 2025), `ch11-benchmarks` (Xu, Guan, Greene and Kechadi; Singh et al. list checked), `ch11-aml` (Notley, Chambers, Park and Dezuanni; DOI added; 4,442 total including boosters, 3,852 representative), and Bastani et al., *PNAS* 122(26), e2422633122.

The pre-lock recheck list in `research/chapter-11-bibliography.md` (items 1–9) was also run; results are recorded there. Item 10, the wording sweep, is a manuscript-lock step and has not been done.
