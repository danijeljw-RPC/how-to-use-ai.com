## Priority 1 — Not in the Research Package; Confirm Against a Primary Source

### 1. Exact wording of the 2023 extinction-risk statement

- [ ] Resolved

Quoted from general knowledge: "Mitigating the risk of extinction from AI should be a global priority alongside other societal-scale risks such as pandemics and nuclear war." Confirm against <https://safe.ai/work/statement-on-ai-extinction-risk>.

### 2. Protein-structure details

- [ ] Resolved

"About fifty years", "in 2020", "more than 200 million proteins", and "shared the Nobel Prize in Chemistry" in 2024. Confirm against the AlphaFold Database and the Nobel press release. The Nobel Prize was shared with computational protein design work; the prose says only that the researchers "shared" it.

### 3. Weather model figures

- [ ] Resolved

"Ten-day forecasts in under a minute" and "about 90 per cent of the measures" (90 per cent of 1,380 verification targets). Confirm against Lam et al., *Science* 2023.

### 4. Expert-survey forecast shift

- [ ] Resolved

"Moved thirteen years earlier between surveys run in 2022 and 2023" (2060 → 2047). The 2047 figure and the 38–51 per cent wording were confirmed from the arXiv abstract on 1 October 2026; confirm the 2060 comparison in the paper body.

### 5. Bar-exam comparison group

- [ ] Resolved

The percentile figures were confirmed via search summaries of Martínez's abstract. Confirm against the paper that the original 90th-percentile figure was based on a February administration skewed towards repeat takers.

### 6. Headline and press-release studies

- [ ] Resolved

"More than 100,000 headline variations" (Robertson et al. 2023) and "much more likely to exaggerate" (Sumner et al. 2014). Confirm numbers and wording against the papers.

### 7. Chess history

- [ ] Resolved

"In 1958 … within ten years … world's chess champion" and "1997 … beat the reigning world champion in a match". Confirm against the National Academies source.

### 8. Builder.ai newsletter walk-back

- [ ] Resolved

"One widely read technical newsletter, after speaking to people who had worked there, publicly walked back the strongest form of the story." Confirm the sequence against The Pragmatic Engineer issue cited in `ch11-builder`.
