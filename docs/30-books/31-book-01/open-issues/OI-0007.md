# OI-0007 — Chapter 1 Reflection Needs the Actual Spotify Song

## Status

Open

## Date Opened

2026-10-03

## Area

Book 1

## Question

Which artist or song did Spotify introduce the author to in the Chapter 1 reflection?

## Why This Matters

The Chapter 1 author reflection (GitHub issue #27) was added word for word on 2026-10-03. The author's text left a slot for the real example:

> One example that stuck with me was **[insert the actual artist/song Spotify introduced you to]**.

The rest of the paragraph depends on it ("Different artist, different corner of music…"). A real, specific song is what makes the story credible. A made-up one would break the project rule against inventing author experiences, so no song has been filled in.

## Current Assumptions

- The slot now reads `[Author input needed: insert the actual artist/song Spotify introduced you to]`, and the paragraph is wrapped in `<!-- AUTHOR-INPUT id="CH01-1" status="required" -->`. A release build (`publish-draft-books.sh book 1 --release`) treats it as an unresolved placeholder and stops. Draft and proof builds still print the bracketed text.
- Everything else in the reflection is unchanged.

## Options

### Option 1 — Supply the real artist and song

Pros:

- Completes the story as the author intended.

Cons:

- None, if the author remembers it.

### Option 2 — Rewrite the sentence without naming the song

For example, "One example that stuck with me was a song I would never have gone looking for."

Pros:

- Works if the author can't remember the exact track.

Cons:

- Less vivid. The wording change is the author's call.

## Recommended Next Step

The author supplies the artist and song (or chooses Option 2). Replace the bracketed slot, remove the `AUTHOR-INPUT` comment lines around the paragraph, and close this OI.
