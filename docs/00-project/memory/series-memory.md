# Series Memory

## Series Name

How-to-use-ai.com book series.

## Current Assumptions

- The series is aimed at beginners.
- Book 1 is non-technical.
- The series should support future website and video adaptation.
- The author wants an approachable but technically trustworthy style.
- Mermaid diagrams are useful when they add value.
- The series should avoid hype and avoid treating AI as magic.

## Meaning of “Book”

When project files say “book”, they usually mean the full series unless a specific volume is named.

## Durable Style Direction

The series should feel like a crossover between:

- O’Reilly-style structure and technical trust
- Dummies-style accessibility and practicality

## Known Audience Balance for Book 1

- 65% consumer
- 35% professional

## Known Reader Baseline for Book 1

The reader may have never used AI before.
