## Status

## Date Opened

## Question

What is the confirmed title (and subtitle) for Book 1?

## Why This Matters

ADR-02-0001 uses **AI for Normal People** as a working title throughout the structural decision, and chapter drafting will eventually reference the title in front matter, marketing copy, and cross-book references. The title is currently only "proposed," not confirmed.

## Current Assumptions

- Working title: **AI for Normal People**
- Working subtitle: **Understanding Artificial Intelligence Without the Hype**
- This is used as a placeholder in planning documents only; it has not been used inside chapter body text.

## Options

### Option 1 — AI for Normal People (current working title)

Pros:

- Signals non-technical, approachable, practical positioning immediately.
- Not written for engineers — matches the 65/35 consumer/professional balance.

Cons:

- "Normal" could read as slightly othering to some readers.

### Option 2 — How to Use AI (legacy working title from `legacy-data/how-to-use-ai-book-chat-handoff.md`)

Pros:

- Matches the project/domain name (how-to-use-ai.com) exactly.
- Direct, searchable, broad, memorable.

Cons:

- Sounds more like a manual than a literacy book; less distinctive shelf positioning.

### Option 3 — Other alternatives surfaced in legacy planning

From `legacy-data/book1_ai_literacy_context_reference.md`:

- The Beginner's Guide to AI
- Everyday AI
- AI Without the Buzzwords
- AI Explained Like You're Human
- Artificial Intelligence for Everyone

Pros:

- More options to commercially test.

Cons:

- None evaluated in depth yet; would need a fresh comparison pass.
