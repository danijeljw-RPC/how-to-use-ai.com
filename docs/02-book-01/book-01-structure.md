# Book 1 Structure

## Working Purpose

Book 1 introduces AI to non-technical readers who may have never used AI before.

## Confirmed Chapter Direction

### Chapter 1 — You’ve Already Been Using AI

Summary:

Introduce the idea that AI is already everywhere:

- Netflix recommendations
- Google Maps
- spam filters
- TikTok algorithms
- Siri and Alexa
- banking fraud detection
- online shopping recommendations

Purpose:

- remove intimidation
- show AI is not science fiction
- explain AI as “software that finds patterns and makes predictions”

Core takeaway:

> AI is not magic. It is pattern recognition at scale.
