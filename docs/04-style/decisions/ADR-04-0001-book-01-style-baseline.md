# ADR-04-0001 — Book 1 Style Baseline

## Status

Proposed

## Date

2026-05-18

## Area

Style

## Context

Book 1 is intended for readers who may have never used AI before. The author wants the book to be approachable and conversational while still having a clear technical purpose.

Existing project direction says Book 1 should:

- be truly non-technical
- help beginners get a foot in the door
- balance consumer and professional relevance
- use a conversational tone
- use diagrams where they add value
- use some humour where appropriate
- feel like a crossover between O’Reilly and Dummies

## Decision

Use a beginner-first style for Book 1.

The style should be:

- conversational
- practical
- clear
- non-hype
- technically trustworthy
- approachable
- lightly humorous only when useful

Explain AI as:

> software that finds patterns and makes predictions

Use the baseline takeaway:

> AI is not magic. It is pattern recognition at scale.

## Options Considered

### Option 1 — Beginner-first conversational technical style

Pros:

- matches the intended Book 1 audience
- reduces intimidation
- supports consumer and professional readers
- allows technical ideas to be introduced gradually
- works well for book, website, and video adaptation

Cons:

- requires discipline to avoid oversimplification
- may need later sidebars for technically advanced readers
- requires careful editing to avoid sounding too casual

### Option 2 — Formal technical style

Pros:

- precise
- familiar to technical readers
- easier to structure academically

Cons:

- mismatches the beginner audience
- may intimidate readers
- less suitable for broad consumer adoption
- not aligned with Book 1 goals

### Option 3 — Highly casual mainstream style

Pros:

- approachable
- easy to read quickly
- may suit a general audience

Cons:

- risks losing technical trust
- may become vague
- may age poorly
- may weaken professional relevance

## Consequences

Positive:

- Book 1 has a clear voice.
- Readers can enter without technical background.
- Chapters can explain practical AI use without hype.
- The style can later be adapted into website and video content.

Negative or trade-offs:

- The manuscript will need careful review for precision.
- Advanced topics may need to be deferred to later books.
- Some technical readers may want deeper detail than Book 1 provides.
