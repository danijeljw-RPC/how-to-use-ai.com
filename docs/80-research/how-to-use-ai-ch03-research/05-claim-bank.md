# Source-Backed Claim Bank

Short claims for adaptation into Chapter 3. These are **not** intended to be pasted unchanged as a wall of statistics.

---

## General capability

**Claim:** Current AI systems can perform well across language, coding, reasoning, image/video and agentic tasks, but performance remains uneven across tasks.

Sources:

- Stanford HAI 2026 Technical Performance: https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index/2026-ai-index-report/technical-performance
- HBS jagged frontier: https://aiinstitute.hbs.edu/navigating-the-jagged-technological-frontier/

**Safe phrasing:** “Modern AI is broad but uneven: it can be excellent at one task and surprisingly weak at another.”

---

## Writing

**Claim:** In a 2023 randomized experiment on mid-level professional writing tasks, AI access reduced average completion time by 40% and raised assessed output quality by 18%.

Source: https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adh2586

**Safe phrasing:** “Controlled experiments show that AI can materially speed up some professional writing tasks while improving the first-pass output.”

**Do not say:** “AI makes writing 40% faster.”

---

## Customer support / knowledge transfer

**Claim:** A large customer-support study found average productivity gains from AI assistance, with larger gains among less experienced/lower-skilled workers.

Source: https://www.gsb.stanford.edu/faculty-research/publications/generative-ai-work

**Safe phrasing:** “AI can sometimes narrow experience gaps by making useful patterns or examples available to less-experienced workers.”

---

## Knowledge work

**Claim:** A large Microsoft field experiment found AI access changed email/document work and produced measurable time savings in some tasks.

Source: https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/shifting-work-patterns-with-generative-ai/

**Safe phrasing:** “The value of generative AI is not confined to demos; field experiments have measured changes in real workplace tasks.”

---

## Coding — positive evidence

**Claim:** Multiple controlled/field experiments have found faster task completion or higher completed-task output with AI coding assistance in specific settings.

Sources:

- https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.06590
- https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/the-effects-of-generative-ai-on-high-skilled-work-evidence-from-three-field-experiments-with-software-developers/
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.12944

**Safe phrasing:** “AI coding assistants can deliver substantial gains on some tasks and for some developers.”

---

## Coding — counterevidence

**Claim:** In a METR randomized study of experienced maintainers working on mature repositories they already knew well, early-2025 AI tools increased completion time by 19%.

Source: https://metr.org/blog/2025-07-10-early-2025-ai-experienced-os-dev-study/

**Safe phrasing:** “AI assistance is not automatically faster; in at least one realistic study of experienced maintainers, it slowed work.”

**Important:** Also state that the authors caution against generalising the result to all software development.

---

## Creativity

**Claim:** Experimental studies have found that AI assistance can improve rated creativity or idea quality.

Sources:

- https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-024-01953-1
- https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-53303-w

**Safe phrasing:** “AI can be a useful creative partner, particularly for generating or expanding possibilities.”

---

## Creativity diversity

**Claim:** Other research finds AI-assisted groups can converge on more similar ideas, reducing diversity even when individual outputs improve.

Sources:

- https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-025-02173-x
- https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S294988212500091X
- https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2949882126000538

**Safe phrasing:** “AI may improve an individual idea while making a crowd of users more similar if everyone draws from the same kinds of suggestions.”

---

## Tutoring

**Claim:** A 2025 RCT in undergraduate physics found students using a purpose-built, pedagogy-informed AI tutor learned more in less time than a comparison active-learning class.

Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-97652-6

**Safe phrasing:** “Carefully designed AI tutoring can produce strong learning results in some settings.”

**Do not say:** “AI is better than teachers.”

---

## Translation

**Claim:** Research shows strong LLM translation ability but uneven quality across languages/domains, with human experts still outperforming in some comparisons.

Sources:

- https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.03658
- https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-naacl.176/
- https://aclanthology.org/2024.wmt-1.28/

**Safe phrasing:** “AI translation can be impressively fluent, but quality depends on language, context and specialist terminology.”

---

## Summarisation

**Claim:** Factual inconsistency in LLM-generated summaries remains an active research problem.

Sources:

- https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-acl.841/
- https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-acl.597/
- https://aclanthology.org/2025.naacl-short.38/
- https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.648/

**Safe phrasing:** “Summarisation is useful and fast, but important summaries should be checked against their source.”

---

## Voice

**Claim:** Large-scale speech-recognition models demonstrate robust multilingual transcription and speech translation, while newer speech-language models increasingly combine understanding and generation.

Sources:

- https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.04356
- https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.682/

**Safe phrasing:** “Voice is now another practical interface to AI: systems can transcribe speech, understand spoken requests and generate spoken responses.”

---

## Automation and agents

**Claim:** Agentic systems improved rapidly through 2025, but current benchmark results still show meaningful failure rates.

Sources:

- https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index/2026-ai-index-report/technical-performance
- https://metr.org/time-horizons/

**Safe phrasing:** “AI can increasingly use tools and carry out multi-step workflows, but autonomy is not the same as reliability.”

---

## Probabilistic language generation

**Claim:** Language models assign probability distributions over possible next tokens/words and generate sequences from those learned distributions.

Source: https://web.stanford.edu/~jurafsky/slp3/

**Safe phrasing:** “A language model builds a response piece by piece, using learned patterns to estimate plausible continuations.”

**Do not say:** “It looks up the answer.”

---

## Hallucinations / confident incorrect output

**Claim:** Large language models can produce plausible false statements; research links this to statistical properties of pretraining and to incentives that reward guessing rather than abstaining.

Sources:

- https://openai.com/index/why-language-models-hallucinate/
- https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10549-w
- https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-generative-artificial-intelligence

**Safe phrasing:** “Fluent language is not a built-in fact checker. A model can produce the shape of a convincing answer even when a detail is wrong.”

---

## Critical thinking

**Claim:** A CHI 2025 survey of knowledge workers found higher confidence in AI was associated with less self-reported critical thinking; workers described their thinking shifting toward verification, integration and stewardship.

Source: https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/the-impact-of-generative-ai-on-critical-thinking-self-reported-reductions-in-cognitive-effort-and-confidence-effects-from-a-survey-of-knowledge-workers/

**Safe phrasing:** “AI can move cognitive effort from producing a first pass to checking, integrating and judging it.”

**Do not say:** “AI makes people stupid” or “AI destroys critical thinking.”

---

# Five especially useful chapter sentences

These are synthesis statements, not quotations:

1. **AI is broad, but its capability is jagged rather than uniform.**
2. **A good AI task is often one where generating, transforming or interpreting information is expensive for you but easy to check afterwards.**
3. **The best AI workflows do not remove human judgement; they move it to the parts that matter most.**
4. **A language model generates a plausible continuation, not a guarantee.**
5. **Confidence is a writing style; correctness is something you still have to establish.**
