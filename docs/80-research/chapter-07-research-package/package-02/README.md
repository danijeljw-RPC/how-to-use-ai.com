## Core editorial proposition being tested

> AI can multiply professional capability, but it does not inherit the worker's accountability, organisational responsibilities, expertise, or judgement.

The evidence supports this proposition as a useful **general professional principle**, but only if it is kept narrower than a universal productivity claim. Research repeatedly shows that outcomes depend on the task, worker, model/tool, workflow, information supplied, and review process.

## Evidence labels used

- **Controlled experiment / RCT:** stronger evidence for causal effects in the studied setting, but not automatically generalisable to every occupation.
- **Field experiment / deployment study:** useful real-work evidence; inspect organisation, tool, task and sponsorship.
- **Survey:** useful for adoption and reported experience; usually self-reported rather than measured productivity.
- **Regulator/government guidance:** authoritative for the jurisdiction and audience described; not universal legal advice.
- **Professional-body guidance:** useful evidence of professional expectations in that field; not a rule for every occupation.
- **Vendor documentation:** authoritative for current product behaviour/terms, not independent evidence that a product improves work.
- **Research synthesis/editorial inference:** an interpretation drawn from several sources; should be presented as an editorial principle rather than a measured fact.

## Time-sensitive material

Vendor privacy, retention, training, product names, licensing and administrative controls can change quickly. All vendor-specific claims in this package should be checked again before publication. The source catalogue records the product/account scope and retrieval date where relevant.

## Strong caution for the later writer

Do not turn this chapter into a privacy-law, cybersecurity, governance, employment-futures, or enterprise-architecture chapter. The research supports a practical chapter whose majority is about ordinary work, with confidentiality, organisational policy and verification acting as concise professional guardrails.
