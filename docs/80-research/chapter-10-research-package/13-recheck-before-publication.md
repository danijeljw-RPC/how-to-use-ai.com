## Study-version caveat

Any study tied to:
- GPT-4;
- Claude 3.x;
- Cursor/Copilot 2025;
- early chatbot interfaces;

should be described as evidence about a specific period and setup, not as a timeless measure of "AI capability."

## Recommended publication-date note

The final book may benefit from a small note such as:

> Labour-market evidence around generative AI is changing quickly. Figures in this chapter are dated in the source notes; the companion website maintains newer material.

This would reduce pressure to make print claims sound permanent.
