# Labour economics and skills

## Deming — The Growing Importance of Social Skills in the Labor Market
- **Author:** David J. Deming
- **Date:** 2017
- **Source type:** Peer-reviewed labour-economics paper, *Quarterly Journal of Economics*
- **URL:** https://academic.oup.com/qje/article-abstract/132/4/1593/3861633
- **Supports:** US employment shifted toward occupations requiring social interaction from 1980–2012; combinations of social and mathematical skills performed especially strongly.
- **Usefulness:** Core evidence that interpersonal skills have had measurable labour-market value rather than being merely employer rhetoric.
- **Limitations:** Historical US data; predates GenAI; occupation-level skill measures are proxies.
- **Recheck:** No, except for later follow-up evidence.

## Deming & Kahn — Skill Requirements across Firms and Labor Markets
- **Authors:** David J. Deming; Lisa B. Kahn
- **Date:** 2017
- **Source type:** NBER working paper / labour-economics study
- **URL:** https://www.nber.org/papers/w23328
- **Supports:** Cognitive and social skill requirements in professional job postings and evidence of complementarity.
- **Limitations:** Job-posting requirements are not identical to actual worker skill or causal wage effects.
- **Recheck:** Low priority.

## Autor — Applying AI to Rebuild Middle Class Jobs
- **Author:** David Autor
- **Date:** 2024
- **Source type:** NBER working paper / economic argument
- **URL:** https://www.nber.org/papers/w32140
- **Supports:** Argument that AI could extend access to expertise and enable more workers to perform higher-stakes tasks.
- **Limitations:** Forward-looking argument, not proof of future distributional effects.
- **Recheck:** Medium.

## Agrawal, Gans & Goldfarb — How AI Will Change Strategy
- **Authors:** Ajay Agrawal; Joshua Gans; Avi Goldfarb
- **Date:** 2017
- **Source type:** Economic framework / HBR article
- **URL:** https://hbr.org/2017/10/how-ai-will-change-strategy-a-thought-experiment
- **Supports:** Cheap prediction can raise the value of complementary inputs such as judgement.
- **Limitations:** Conceptual economic argument; not direct causal evidence on wages or occupations.
- **Recheck:** No for concept, yes for modern examples.

# Generative AI at work and domain expertise

## Brynjolfsson, Li & Raymond — Generative AI at Work
- **Authors:** Erik Brynjolfsson; Danielle Li; Lindsey R. Raymond
- **Date:** 2023 working paper; later journal publication
- **Source type:** Large field study
- **URL:** https://www.nber.org/papers/w31161
- **Supports:** 5,179 customer-support agents; AI assistance improved productivity, with larger gains for novice/lower-skilled workers.
- **Limitations:** Specific company/task/tool; immediate productivity is not identical to durable skill acquisition.
- **Recheck:** Medium.

## Dell'Acqua et al. — Navigating the Jagged Technological Frontier
- **Authors:** Fabrizio Dell'Acqua et al.
- **Date:** 2023
- **Source type:** Experimental field study / SSRN working paper
- **URL:** https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.4573321
- **Supports:** AI can improve professional performance inside its capability frontier and worsen performance when workers rely on it outside that frontier.
- **Limitations:** Specific GPT-4 era and consultant tasks; model frontier changes rapidly.
- **Recheck:** **Yes.**

## Tambe — Reskilling Workforce for AI: Domain Expertise and Algorithmic Literacy
- **Author:** Prasanna Tambe
- **Date:** 2025 publication
- **Source type:** Peer-reviewed management/AI workforce research
- **URL:** https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mnsc.2022.03968
- **Supports:** Complementarity between domain expertise and algorithmic literacy; useful against generic "AI skill replaces expertise" narratives.
- **Limitations:** Context-specific empirical design; chapter should not universalise magnitude.
- **Recheck:** Medium.

## Microsoft Research — Shifting Work Patterns with Generative AI
- **Organisation:** Microsoft Research
- **Date:** 2025
- **Source type:** Large randomized workplace experiment
- **URL:** https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/shifting-work-patterns-with-generative-ai/
- **Supports:** GenAI can change time allocation in real knowledge work, including less time on email in the study.
- **Limitations:** Product/ecosystem-specific; Microsoft is an interested party; time savings do not prove skill effects.
- **Recheck:** **Yes.**

# Human-AI decision-making, judgement and automation

## Vaccaro, Almaatouq & Malone — Human-AI combinations meta-analysis
- **Authors:** Michelle Vaccaro; Abdullah Almaatouq; Thomas W. Malone
- **Date:** 2024
- **Source type:** Preregistered systematic review/meta-analysis, *Nature Human Behaviour*
- **URL:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-024-02024-1
- **Supports:** Across 106 studies/370 effect sizes, human-AI combinations do not automatically outperform the better human or AI alone; effects vary by task.
- **Limitations:** Heterogeneous tasks and systems; aggregate effects do not describe every workflow.
- **Recheck:** Medium.

## Cecil et al. — Incorrect AI advice and over-reliance
- **Date:** 2024
- **Source type:** Five preregistered experiments, *Scientific Reports*
- **URL:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-60220-5
- **Supports:** Incorrect AI advice can degrade personnel-selection decisions; explanation did not reliably remove over-reliance.
- **Limitations:** Experimental selection tasks and participant populations; not all high-stakes domains.
- **Recheck:** Medium.

## Sele & Chugunova — Delegation to algorithms
- **Authors:** Daniela Sele; Marina Chugunova
- **Date:** 2024
- **Source type:** Incentivised experiment, *PLOS ONE*
- **URL:** https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0298037
- **Supports:** People can prefer algorithmic delegation and do not always use human-in-the-loop control optimally.
- **Limitations:** Experimental setting; algorithm preferences vary strongly by context.
- **Recheck:** Low-medium.

## Bainbridge — Ironies of Automation
- **Author:** Lisanne Bainbridge
- **Date:** 1983
- **Source type:** Foundational automation/human-factors paper, *Automatica*
- **URL:** https://doi.org/10.1016/0005-1098(83)90046-8
- **Supports:** Automation can leave humans with rare, difficult supervision/failure tasks while reducing routine practice.
- **Limitations:** Predates modern AI; conceptual/human-factors transfer must be explained rather than assumed identical.
- **Recheck:** No.

# Cognitive offloading, learning and deskilling

## Microsoft Research / Carnegie Mellon — Impact of GenAI on Critical Thinking
- **Date:** CHI 2025
- **Source type:** Survey of 319 knowledge workers / 936 reported GenAI uses
- **URL:** https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/the-impact-of-generative-ai-on-critical-thinking-self-reported-reductions-in-cognitive-effort-and-confidence-effects-from-a-survey-of-knowledge-workers/
- **Supports:** AI confidence associated with lower self-reported critical-thinking effort; critical work shifts toward verification/integration/stewardship.
- **Limitations:** Self-report; cannot establish objective skill decline or causal effect.
- **Recheck:** **Yes.**

## Bastani et al. — Generative AI without guardrails can harm learning
- **Authors:** Hamsa Bastani et al.
- **Date:** 2025
- **Source type:** Randomized classroom experiment, *PNAS*
- **URL:** https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2422633122
- **Supports:** Unrestricted GPT improved assisted maths practice but harmed later unaided performance relative to control; guardrailed tutor produced a different learning outcome.
- **Limitations:** High-school maths in Türkiye; does not directly establish adult workplace effects.
- **Recheck:** Medium.

## Budzyń et al. — AI exposure and unaided adenoma detection
- **Date:** 2025
- **Source type:** Multicentre observational clinical study, *Lancet Gastroenterology & Hepatology*
- **URL:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40816301/
- **Supports:** Unaided detection was lower after routine AI-exposure period, consistent with possible deskilling.
- **Limitations:** Observational; causality not established; mixed evidence exists.
- **Recheck:** **Yes.**

## Prospective endoscopy counter-evidence
- **Date:** 2026
- **Source type:** Prospective clinical study
- **URL:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13552228/
- **Supports:** Under different participants/design, temporary AI assistance did not produce significant persistent unaided deskilling.
- **Limitations:** Specific inexperienced endoscopists and study protocol.
- **Recheck:** **Yes.**

## MIT Media Lab — Your Brain on ChatGPT
- **Date:** 2025
- **Source type:** Preprint / EEG essay-writing study
- **URL:** https://www.media.mit.edu/publications/your-brain-on-chatgpt/
- **Supports:** Early evidence/questions about neural engagement, recall and ownership under different writing tools.
- **Limitations:** Small sample, preprint, narrow task, methodological/interpretive criticism; easy to overstate.
- **Recheck:** **Mandatory.** Use only if peer-review/replication status improves.

# AI performance on "human" tasks

## Ayers et al. — Physician versus chatbot responses
- **Authors:** John W. Ayers et al.
- **Date:** 2023
- **Source type:** Peer-reviewed comparative evaluation, *JAMA Internal Medicine*
- **URL:** https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamainternalmedicine/fullarticle/2804309
- **Supports:** 195 online patient questions; evaluators preferred chatbot responses and rated them higher for quality/empathy in the study.
- **Limitations:** Text-only retrospective comparison, not clinical outcomes; AI answers longer; evaluator perception is not felt empathy.
- **Recheck:** **Yes** for newer model/replication evidence.

## Strachan et al. — Testing theory of mind in LLMs and humans
- **Date:** 2024
- **Source type:** Peer-reviewed experimental comparison, *Nature Human Behaviour*
- **URL:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-024-01882-z
- **Supports:** GPT-4 matched/exceeded human performance on several theory-of-mind-style tasks while showing distinct weaknesses.
- **Limitations:** Benchmark performance does not establish consciousness or durable social understanding.
- **Recheck:** **Yes.**

## LLM persuasion experiments
- **Date:** 2025
- **Source type:** Three preregistered experiments, *Nature Communications*
- **URL:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-61345-5
- **Supports:** LLM-generated persuasive messages can measurably shift attitudes in bounded experimental settings and perform comparably to lay humans.
- **Limitations:** Experimental messages/attitudes; not universal durable behaviour change.
- **Recheck:** **Yes.**

## Lee & Chung — AI-assisted creative ideation
- **Date:** 2024
- **Source type:** Five experiments, *Nature Human Behaviour*
- **URL:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-024-01953-1
- **Supports:** GPT assistance can improve judged creativity on several ideation tasks.
- **Limitations:** Bounded tests; not a full account of creativity/craft/cultural value.
- **Recheck:** Medium-high.

## Sourati, Karimi-Malekabadi & Dehghani — Linguistic diversity under LLM rewriting
- **Date:** 2026
- **Source type:** Peer-reviewed multi-dataset study, *Nature Human Behaviour*
- **URL:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-026-02550-0
- **Supports:** LLM rewriting can preserve content while reducing variation in writing complexity and amplifying dominant features.
- **Limitations:** Model/prompt/data dependent; very recent.
- **Recheck:** **Mandatory.**

# Communication, disclosure and trust

## Source disclosure and AI-generated messages
- **Date:** 2024
- **Source type:** Experimental communication research, *Computers in Human Behavior: Artificial Humans*
- **URL:** https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2949882124000185
- **Supports:** Disclosure of AI authorship can change message evaluation, with effects moderated by attitudes/context.
- **Limitations:** Specific prevention-message context; modest effects should not be generalised.
- **Recheck:** Medium.

## AI-attributed corporate apologies
- **Date:** 2025
- **Source type:** Experimental public-relations/communication research
- **URL:** https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0363811124000997
- **Supports:** AI attribution can reduce perceived sincerity/forgiveness in the tested apology setting.
- **Limitations:** Simulated scenario; not every relationship or message type.
- **Recheck:** Medium.

## Sim, Kim & Ku — ChatGPT role-play in pragmatics learning
- **Date:** 2025
- **Source type:** Randomized educational comparison, *TESOL Quarterly*
- **URL:** https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/tesq.70010
- **Supports:** AI role-play can support short-term learning while peer interaction can differ in retention; useful model for AI as practice partner.
- **Limitations:** N=40, Korean EFL learners, specific speech acts.
- **Recheck:** Medium.

## Eguchi et al. — Human versus AI role-play interaction
- **Date:** 2025
- **Source type:** Applied conversation-analysis study, *TESOL Quarterly*
- **URL:** https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/tesq.70028
- **Supports:** AI role-play can elicit interactional competence but differs from human turn-taking.
- **Limitations:** Language-learning/assessment context, not general relationships.
- **Recheck:** Medium.

# Systems thinking

## Meadows — Leverage Points: Places to Intervene in a System
- **Author:** Donella Meadows
- **Source type:** Foundational systems-thinking essay
- **URL:** https://donellameadows.org/archives/leverage-points-places-to-intervene-in-a-system/
- **Supports:** Beginner explanations of feedback, stocks/flows, delays, rules and leverage points.
- **Limitations:** Conceptual, not experimental validation of systems thinking as an individual trait.
- **Recheck:** No.

## Development and Validation of the Systems Thinking Scale
- **Source type:** Psychometric-development study
- **URL:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7403244/
- **Supports:** Example of an attempt to operationalise/measure systems thinking and acknowledgement of limited empirical measurement literature.
- **Limitations:** Instrument/domain-specific; not proof of universal validity.
- **Recheck:** Medium.

## Research trends on systems thinking approach in science education
- **Date:** 2023
- **Source type:** Systematic review
- **URL:** https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/09500693.2023.2245106
- **Supports:** Inconsistent definitions/characteristics across systems-thinking education research.
- **Limitations:** Science-education literature, not adult workplace performance.
- **Recheck:** Low-medium.

## Systems Thinking in Public Health Education: A Scoping Review
- **Date:** 2026
- **Source type:** Scoping review
- **URL:** https://www.mdpi.com/2079-8954/14/9/1115
- **Supports:** Teaching often uses mapping, simulation and experiential methods; definitions/validated assessment remain inconsistent.
- **Limitations:** Only 13 studies in a specific domain; scoping rather than effect meta-analysis.
- **Recheck:** Medium.

# Adaptability, adult learning and growth mindset

## OECD — Trends in Adult Learning
- **Organisation:** OECD
- **Date:** 2025
- **Source type:** International adult-learning report
- **URL:** https://doi.org/10.1787/ec0624a6-en
- **Supports:** Participation patterns, employer support, adult-learning barriers and the need for continuing learning.
- **Limitations:** Cross-national averages; participation is not equivalent to effective learning.
- **Recheck:** Medium.

## OECD — Education Policy Outlook 2025
- **Organisation:** OECD
- **Date:** 2025
- **Source type:** Education-policy synthesis
- **URL:** https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/education-policy-outlook-2025_c3f402ba-en.html
- **Supports:** Lifelong-learning policy and conditions for mid-career/older learners.
- **Limitations:** Policy synthesis rather than intervention meta-analysis.
- **Recheck:** Medium.

## Macnamara & Burgoyne — Growth mindset intervention meta-analysis
- **Date:** 2023
- **Source type:** Meta-analysis, *Psychological Bulletin*
- **URL:** https://doi.org/10.1037/bul0000352
- **Supports:** Very small overall academic-achievement effects; strongest-quality subset not statistically significant after relevant adjustments.
- **Limitations:** Focuses academic achievement, not every outcome called adaptability; part of an active scholarly debate.
- **Recheck:** Low-medium.

## Burnette et al. — Growth mindset intervention meta-analysis
- **Date:** 2023
- **Source type:** Meta-analysis
- **URL:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36227318/
- **Supports:** Benefits appear larger in some targeted/high-fidelity populations and outcomes, highlighting heterogeneity.
- **Limitations:** Interpretation contested; not a blanket endorsement of growth-mindset programmes.
- **Recheck:** Low-medium.
