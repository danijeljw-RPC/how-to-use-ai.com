# N. Source-use priorities for the later writer

## Highest-value primary/authoritative sources to cite in print

1. International AI Safety Report 2026 — serious risk + uncertainty.
2. SEC AI-washing enforcement — documented commercial overclaiming.
3. FTC AI enforcement — documented consumer overclaiming.
4. Australian Treasury ACL review — Australian regulatory relevance.
5. ASIC REP 798 — Australian operational/governance evidence.
6. AlphaFold Nature paper — genuine breakthrough.
7. PNAS education field experiment — assisted performance vs learning.
8. Martínez bar-exam analysis — benchmark/comparator lesson.
9. Wineburg/McGrew — lateral reading.
10. Google developer Gemini demo explanation — demo framing.

## Strong but time-sensitive/current sources

- Stanford AI Index 2026;
- Waymo operational status;
- Australian AI Safety Institute;
- McKinsey enterprise surveys;
- METR developer productivity;
- Project NANDA report;
- latest expert surveys.

## Sources best kept primarily for the companion website

- Tesla promotional-video investigation unless final primary findings are available;
- detailed Builder.ai allegations/investigations;
- Rabbit/Humane review comparisons;
- volatile leaderboard rankings;
- named executive forecasts with short timelines;
- current investment/bubble figures.
