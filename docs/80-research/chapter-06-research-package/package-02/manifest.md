## Quality controls

- Company sources are labelled as company sources.
- Survey/observational evidence is not presented as causal evidence.
- U.S. data are not presented as Australian prevalence.
- Product features are treated as time-sensitive.
- Free TAFE cumulative counts are not misrepresented as a cohort completion rate.
- Broad claims such as “AI makes people stupid” are explicitly rejected as unsupported.
- Contrasting evidence is retained where useful, including Pew versus Google's different click/traffic measures.
- A retracted 2025 ChatGPT learning meta-analysis is explicitly flagged as unusable.
- Over-reliance research is marked as supporting material so it does not overwhelm the practical purpose of Chapter 6.
