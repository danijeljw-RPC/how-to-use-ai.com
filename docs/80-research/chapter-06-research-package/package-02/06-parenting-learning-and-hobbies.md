# Parenting Support, Children's Learning and Hobbies

The strongest research-backed distinction is:

> AI can support learning, but completing a task successfully with AI is not automatically the same thing as learning to perform it without AI.

# Parenting support

## Appropriate uses

- explain a school concept to a parent;
- suggest an age-appropriate explanation;
- generate practice questions;
- provide hints;
- help a parent understand homework;
- prepare questions for a teacher;
- brainstorm activities;
- organise a study timetable;
- generate reading questions.

The parent remains responsible for judging suitability and knowing their own child.

## Australian framework

The Australian Framework for Generative AI in Schools is organised around six principles:

1. Teaching and Learning
2. Human and Social Wellbeing
3. Transparency
4. Fairness
5. Accountability
6. Privacy, Security and Safety

The framework is valuable because it does not frame GenAI as automatically prohibited or automatically beneficial.

The Australian Curriculum also distinguishes AI language models from search engines and emphasises understanding what AI systems can and cannot do.

## Australian children's current use

The eSafety Commissioner surveyed 1,950 Australian children aged 10–17 in 2026.

Reported findings include:
- 78% had used an AI assistant;
- among users, 20% used AI assistants/companions daily or more;
- 91% reported functional uses such as information, learning or translation;
- 70% reported writing/editing use;
- 54% reported at least one personal/social use;
- 32% had shared personal or potentially sensitive information;
- 20% had experienced potentially inappropriate or harmful interactions.

These figures show AI is already part of children's lives. Chapter 6 should not turn them into a full child-safety discussion; that belongs elsewhere.

# Answer mode versus learning mode

## Answer mode
> Give me the answer to question 4.

Benefit:
- immediate progress.

Potential learning weakness:
- little retrieval or reasoning practice.

## Explanation mode
> Explain the idea I need before I can solve question 4.

## Hint mode
> Give me one hint. Don't solve it.

## Tutor mode
> Ask me one question at a time. If I am wrong, explain my mistake and let me try again.

This is an accessible way to show that interaction style changes what the user gets from AI.

# Evidence: tutoring can help, but design matters

## Kestin et al., Scientific Reports, 2025

A randomised controlled trial in an authentic university physics setting found strong learning outcomes from a carefully designed AI tutor compared with the in-class active-learning comparison condition.

Important qualification:
- this does not mean an unguided general chatbot produces the same result;
- the system was designed around pedagogy.

## Khanmigo trial, NBER 2026

A two-year cluster-randomised trial across 18 Tennessee middle schools found modest math gains from assignment to Khanmigo—around 0.06–0.08 standard deviations over a school year—but actual engagement was limited.

This is useful counterevidence to “AI tutors transform learning automatically”.

## Structured AI support, NBER 2026

A randomised field experiment involving more than 6,000 middle-school students found structured AI support after mistakes improved next-attempt correctness and reduced the attempts needed to return to a correct answer. Students spent longer on supported questions and progressed more slowly.

Editorial lesson:
**faster is not always better when the objective is learning.**

# Retrieval practice

Established pre-GenAI learning research shows active retrieval can improve later retention.

A practical household use:
> Quiz me on this material. Wait for my answer before showing the answer. Bring back anything I get wrong later.

This is capability-building rather than answer production.

# Hobbies

## Gardening
AI can explain terminology, compare approaches, generate a seasonal learning plan and assist with image identification.

Verify:
- toxicity;
- pesticides/chemicals;
- local biosecurity;
- region-specific advice.

## Photography
AI can critique composition, explain exposure, create exercises and suggest projects.

Capability question:
> Does the user learn to take the photograph, or only repair it afterwards?

## Music
AI can explain theory, generate drills, quiz notes/chords and structure a short practice routine.

## Cooking
A tutoring interaction:
> Don't give me the recipe immediately. Ask what I know about making a pan sauce, then teach the missing steps.

## Language learning
AI can roleplay, correct, generate dialogues, quiz vocabulary and adapt difficulty.

# Strong chapter structure

Show one hobby in three modes:

1. **Do it for me**
2. **Show me how**
3. **Coach me while I do it**

This is more memorable than an abstract warning about dependency.
