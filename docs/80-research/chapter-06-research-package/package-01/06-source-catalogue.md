## Writing and productivity

### Experimental evidence on the productivity effects of generative artificial intelligence
- **Authors:** Shakked Noy, Whitney Zhang
- **Publication:** *Science*
- **Date:** 13 July 2023
- **URL:** https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adh2586
- **Supports:** In 453 professionals doing writing tasks, ChatGPT reduced average time by 40% and increased rated quality by 18%.
- **Useful for:** Writing emails/messages; “removing friction.”
- **Caveat:** Professional writing tasks, not every consumer-writing scenario.

### Shifting Work Patterns with Generative AI
- **Authors:** Eleanor W. Dillon, Sonia Jaffe, Nicole Immorlica, Christopher T. Stanton
- **Publication:** NBER Working Paper 33795
- **Date:** 2025, revised November 2025
- **URL:** https://www.nber.org/papers/w33795
- **Supports:** Large field experiment across 66 firms / 7,137 workers; active users spent less time on email.
- **Useful for:** Broader evidence that AI can remove writing/admin friction.
- **Caveat:** Workplace setting.

### AI Suggestions Homogenize Writing Toward Western Styles and Diminish Cultural Nuances
- **Authors:** Dhruv Agarwal, Mor Naaman, Aditya Vashistha
- **Publication:** CHI 2025
- **Date:** 25 April 2025
- **URL:** https://doi.org/10.1145/3706598.3713564
- **Supports:** Controlled evidence that AI writing suggestions can shift style/cultural expression.
- **Useful for:** Qualification of “sounds like you.”
- **Caveat:** Specific cross-cultural writing tasks and model setup.

### Homogenizing effect of large language models on creative diversity
- **Publication:** Computers in Human Behavior: Artificial Humans
- **Date:** 2025
- **URL:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chbah.2025.100207
- **Supports:** Across preregistered studies, human essays contributed more new ideas at the collective level than GPT-4-generated essays.
- **Useful for:** Authorship/voice caveat.

### The shrinking landscape of linguistic diversity in the age of large language models
- **Date:** 2026
- **URL:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42637911/
- **Supports:** Large-scale evidence linking LLM-assisted polishing/rewriting to reduced stylistic variance.
- **Useful for:** Writing/voice caveat.

## Meal planning and nutrition

### Can the AI tools ChatGPT and Bard generate energy, macro- and micro-nutrient sufficient meal plans for different dietary patterns?
- **Date:** 2024
- **URL:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39102765/
- **Supports:** Evaluation of 108 meal plans; recurring nutrient/energy shortcomings; general inspiration versus nutritional adequacy distinction.
- **Useful for:** Meal planning limitation.

### AI dietician: Unveiling the accuracy of ChatGPT’s nutritional estimations
- **Date:** 2024
- **URL:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38194819/
- **Supports:** Some nutritional estimates and generated plans were reasonably close within broad bounds, demonstrating capability alongside limited precision.
- **Useful for:** Contrasting evidence; avoid presenting only failures.

### Can ChatGPT provide appropriate meal plans for NCD patients?
- **Date:** 2024
- **URL:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38359704/
- **Supports:** Potential for personalised plans but need for expert supervision in clinical scenarios.
- **Useful for:** Boundary between consumer inspiration and clinical dietetics.

### Artificial Intelligence in Clinical Nutrition: A Descriptive Comparison of ChatGPT- and Dietitian-Planned Diets for Chronic Disease Scenarios
- **Date:** 2025
- **URL:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41054023/
- **Supports:** Deficiencies in energy/nutrient targets and individualisation compared with dietitian plans.
- **Useful for:** Clinical limitation.

### An Analysis of the Nutritional Value of Diets Generated by Large Language Models Under Average User Conditions — 3(LM)Diet Study
- **Date:** 2026
- **URL:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42514431/
- **Supports:** Systematic energy shortfalls and macronutrient issues across several major LLMs.
- **Useful for:** Current evidence that attractive generated diets are not automatically nutritionally correct.

## Over-reliance, trust and critical thinking

### Appropriate reliance on Generative AI: Research synthesis
- **Authors:** Samir Passi, Shipi Dhanorkar, Mihaela Vorvoreanu
- **Organisation:** Microsoft Research
- **Date:** March 2024
- **URL:** https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/appropriate-reliance-on-generative-ai-research-synthesis/
- **Supports:** Appropriate reliance concept; review of roughly 50 papers; both over- and under-reliance matter.
- **Useful for:** Core conceptual framing.

### Trust and reliance on AI — An experimental study on the extent and costs of overreliance on AI
- **Authors:** Artur Klingbeil, Cassandra Grützner, Philipp Schreck
- **Publication:** *Computers in Human Behavior*
- **Date:** 2024
- **URL:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chb.2024.108352
- **Supports:** Participants can follow AI advice against context/their own judgement; behavioural evidence of over-reliance.
- **Useful for:** Over-reliance section.

### The Impact of Generative AI on Critical Thinking
- **Authors:** Hao-Ping Lee et al.
- **Publication:** CHI 2025
- **URL:** https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/the-impact-of-generative-ai-on-critical-thinking-self-reported-reductions-in-cognitive-effort-and-confidence-effects-from-a-survey-of-knowledge-workers/
- **Supports:** Survey of 319 professionals / 936 use cases; higher confidence in GenAI associated with less critical-thinking effort; verification becomes more important.
- **Useful for:** Nuanced over-reliance discussion.
- **Caveat:** Self-reported knowledge-work context.

### Your Brain on ChatGPT: Accumulation of Cognitive Debt when Using an AI Assistant for Essay Writing Task
- **Authors:** Nataliya Kosmyna et al.
- **Organisation:** MIT Media Lab / arXiv
- **Date:** June 2025
- **URL:** https://www.media.mit.edu/publications/your-brain-on-chatgpt/
- **Supports:** Emerging evidence on neural engagement, recall and ownership in AI-assisted essay writing.
- **Useful for:** Optional “emerging research” sidebar.
- **Caveat:** Preprint, small sample, specific essay-writing task. Do not generalise to all AI use.

### ChatGPT as a cognitive crutch: Evidence from a randomized controlled trial on knowledge retention
- **Author:** André Barcaui
- **Date:** 2025
- **URL:** https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2590291125010186
- **Supports:** Lower 45-day retention in an unrestricted ChatGPT study-aid condition than traditional study condition.
- **Useful for:** Learning/skill-retention nuance.
- **Caveat:** Undergraduate learning context.

### The effects of generative AI on productivity, innovation and entrepreneurship
- **Organisation:** OECD
