# AI as collaborator versus AI as substitute

Research different modes of creative AI use.

For example:

### AI as blank-page breaker
Human has the problem and direction; AI supplies possible starting points.

### AI as variation engine
Human supplies source material; AI creates many alternatives.

### AI as production assistant
Human determines the creative direction; AI accelerates repetitive transformations.

### AI as co-generator
AI contributes substantial expressive material that the human selects or modifies.

### AI-led generation
The human provides a relatively short prompt and the model determines most expressive details.

### Automated content generation
Large numbers of outputs are produced with minimal case-by-case human creative involvement.

Investigate whether these different workflows create different:

- authorship;
- creativity;
- quality;
- copyright;
- transparency;
- economic;
- and ethical questions.

This continuum may be more useful than treating work as simply:

> human-made

versus:

> AI-made.

# Contrasting viewpoints

# Depth

The research can be extensive.

Do not artificially restrict it to the expected length of the eventual Chapter 8.

It is preferable for the chapter-writing AI to receive substantially more high-quality research than it ultimately uses.

Do not pad the package with repetitive information.

Prioritise:

2. primary evidence;
3. recent evidence;
4. practical usefulness;
5. strong real-world examples;
6. creator and industry perspectives;
7. meaningful counterarguments;
8. empirical research;
9. jurisdictional accuracy;
10. clear distinction between evidence and opinion;

# Research philosophy

Do not approach this task trying to prove that:

- AI creativity is good;
- AI creativity is bad;
- creators are overreacting;
- technology companies are stealing;
- AI will replace artists;
- AI will never replace artists;
- AI increases creativity;
- AI destroys creativity.

Approach these as questions to investigate.

The research package should contain enough evidence for the later writer to say:

> Here is what we know.

> Here is what people disagree about.

> Here is what the available evidence suggests.

> Here is what remains unresolved.

> Here is what actually matters if you want to use AI creatively yourself.

That is more valuable than producing a predetermined narrative.
