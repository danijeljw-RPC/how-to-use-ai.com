# 10 — Source Catalogue

## Purpose

This is the working bibliography for the Chapter 8 research package. Source IDs (`S01`, `S02`, …) are used throughout the other files so a later writing system can trace a claim back to its strongest available source.

**Research cut-off:** 30 September 2026 (Australia/Adelaide).

A source's presence here does **not** mean the source is neutral or that every claim in it is accepted. Company, union, collecting-society, creator-advocacy and open-culture sources are included where they are primary evidence of their own policies, data, experiences or arguments. Their limitations are recorded.

For legal and policy material, this catalogue is research support, not legal advice. Anything marked **Recheck before publication** is especially time-sensitive.
