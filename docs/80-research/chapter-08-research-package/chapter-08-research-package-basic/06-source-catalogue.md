# L. Sources to use with extra caution

## Industry forecasts
CISAC/PMP projections are useful but are commissioned by creator-rights organisations and model future scenarios.

## Advocacy surveys
Society of Authors / Authors Guild material is important evidence of stakeholder experience and policy positions but should not automatically be generalised to an entire labour market.

## Company metrics
Deezer's upload/fraud/listening statistics are operational company data. They are valuable and concrete but specific to Deezer unless independently replicated.

## Product marketing
Adobe, OpenAI, Canva, Suno and Runway are authoritative about their own terms and product claims, but claims like “commercially safe” are vendor descriptions, not independent legal determinations.
