## Claims that are durable and do not need precise updates

- "Agent" has inconsistent definitions.
- Capability, deployment and adoption are distinct.
- Prompt injection is structurally important for tool-using systems.
- Narrow robotics tends to mature before general-purpose robotics.
- Immediate assisted performance is not identical to learning.
- Healthcare AI is regulated in Australia when it meets medical-device definitions.
- Driver assistance is not the same as driverless automation.
- Societal outcomes are shaped by institutions, laws, organisations and users as well as technical capability.
- Forecasts should be labelled as forecasts and scenarios as scenarios.
