# OI-0003 — Launch Site Homepage SEO Choices

## Status

Resolved — 2026-09-24

## Date Opened

2026-09-24

## Area

Publishing

## Question

Which of the author-facing choices from the 2026-09-24 Seobility check should the launch site adopt?

## Why This Matters

The technical fixes in `docs/03-publishing/plans/site-seo-fixes-plan.md` need no decision. These items do. They change public wording, what the site links to, or how hard a setting is to reverse.

## Decisions Needed

1. **Homepage title.** Proposed: "How To Use AI.com — Plain-English AI Books for Beginners". Alternatives could name the book ("AI for Normal People") instead.
2. **Extra homepage copy.** About 60–100 words to lift the page above 250 words and reuse the H1's terms ("explained", "people", "other things to do"). The author approves the final wording.
3. **External links.** Add one or two genuine outbound references (for example, a reputable plain-English AI explainer) or leave none. Recommendation: leave none unless a link genuinely helps readers.
4. **Social sharing buttons.** Recommendation: decline. They add third-party scripts and tracking that conflict with the privacy page, for a "nice to have" score.
5. **HSTS preload.** Recommendation: plain HSTS without `preload` for now. Preload is hard to undo and would deserve its own ADR.

## Current Assumptions

- The P1 technical fixes (HTTPS redirect, charset, favicon) go ahead as soon as the plan is approved.
- Nothing in this OI is changed until the author answers.

## Resolution

On 2026-09-24 the author told Claude Code to go ahead using its recommendations:

1. Homepage title: "How To Use AI.com — Plain-English AI Books for Beginners" (56 characters).
2. Homepage copy: added a "Who this is for" section of about 100 words, using the everyday examples from Book 1's framing (Netflix, Google Maps, spam filters, bank fraud alerts). The author can revise the wording at any time.
3. External links: none added.
4. Social sharing buttons: declined, to avoid third-party scripts and tracking.
5. HSTS: `max-age=31536000` only, with no `includeSubDomains` and no `preload`. Any move to preload needs its own ADR.
