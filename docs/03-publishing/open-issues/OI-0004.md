# OI-0004 — Remaining National Library Catalogue Metadata for Book 1

## Status

Open

## Date Opened

2026-09-24

## Area

Publishing

## Question

Which values still need to be settled before Book 1's National Library of Australia prepublication and legal deposit details are submitted?

## Why This Matters

ADR-03-0006 sets the publisher, imprint, series, and title hierarchy, but some fields can't be completed yet. The same values also feed the ISBN record, the copyright page, the PDF metadata, the website, and the covers, so they should be settled once and used everywhere.

## Current Assumptions

- Publisher: `RePass Cloud Pty Ltd`. Imprint: `How To Use AI.com`. Series number: 1. Edition: first. Format: eBook — PDF. Year: 2026. Audience: General. Genre: Non-fiction (all per ADR-03-0006).
- Book 1's title is the working title "AI for Normal People", and its subtitle is "Understanding Artificial Intelligence Without the Hype".

## Items to Settle

1. **Final Book 1 title.** "AI for Normal People" is still a working title (ADR-02-0001, `docs/01-series/series-structure.md`).
2. **Exact series title wording.** Use either "How To Use AI.com" (as recommended in the discussion) or "How To Use AI.com Book Series" (the public series name answered in `docs/00-project/plans/next-editorial-phases.md` Q16). It must match what's printed on the title page.
3. **ISBN.** Buy it under `RePass Cloud Pty Ltd`, with one ISBN for each format (PDF now, then softcover, hardcover, and any other ebook formats).
4. **Estimated retail price.** The intended Australian retail price, or $0 if it's free.
5. **Expected publication month.**
6. **Creator life dates.** Decide whether to give a birth year (`YYYY–`) or leave it out.
7. **Copyright holder.** Decide whether the `©` line names the author or RePass Cloud Pty Ltd.
8. **Publisher category.** Confirm which option the NLA form offers for a company.

## Recommended Next Step

The author settles items 1–2 before the title page and copyright page are typeset, and items 3–8 before the NLA form is submitted. Record the answers here and update ADR-03-0006 where they change its Book 1 values.
