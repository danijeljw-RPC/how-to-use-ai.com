# OI-0002 — Approve Preview Edition Wording

## Status

Open

## Date Opened

2026-09-24

## Area

Publishing

## Question

What final wording should the preview edition's notice page and end-of-preview page use?

## Why This Matters

The preview PDF is the public download behind the launch site's preview links (`OI-0001`). The current wording is placeholder copy written during implementation. It has not been approved by the author.

## Current Assumptions

- Notice page: "PREVIEW EDITION — This free preview contains Chapters 1–3 of the book. The text may change before final publication."
- End page: "END OF PREVIEW — Thanks for reading this preview. You have been reading Chapters 1–3 of AI for Normal People. The full book continues with 11 more chapters. Get the full book at how-to-use-ai.com"
- Neither page has a price, release date, or purchase link, because commerce is not yet live.

## Options

### Option 1 — Keep the neutral placeholder

Pros:

- It is accurate whatever the launch and commerce status.

Cons:

- It is generic and gives the reader little reason to buy.

### Option 2 — Author-written copy with a call to action

Pros:

- It matches the book's voice, and it can tease later chapters.

Cons:

- It must be kept consistent with site pricing and launch status.

## Recommended Next Step

The author reviews the generated `dist/02-book-01-preview.pdf` and supplies or approves the wording. The strings are in `scripts/assemble_draft_book.py` (`_notice_page`, `_preview_end_page`).
