# OI-0010 — Hardcover Cover Measurements per Printer

## Status

Open (only needed before a hardcover edition is released)

## Date Opened

2026-10-05

## Area

Publishing

## Question

What cover bleed (turn-in), hinge and board allowance does each printer require for a hardcover case-laminate cover, and which trim sizes and papers does it offer for hardcovers?

## Why This Matters

ADR-03-0012 lets any book add a hardcover edition in `publishing/books.json` (`"type": "print", "binding": "hardcover"`). The cover is built from `series.print.printers.<printer>.bindings.hardcover`:

- `coverBleedInches`: the wrap around the boards on every outside edge;
- `hingeInches`: the hinge gap on each side of the spine;
- `spineAllowanceInches`: the extra spine width the boards add, on top of page count × paper caliper.

These values differ by printer and change over time. They were not checked when the engine was built, so they are left empty rather than guessed. Until they are filled in, `./pub-books.sh book N --release` stops on a hardcover edition and names each missing field. A `--proof` build skips that cover and warns.

## Options

1. Download each printer's hardcover cover template for the book's trim and page count, and copy the measurements into `books.json`.
2. Use `spineWidthOverrideInches` on the paper to match the template's spine exactly if the formula doesn't.

## Next Step

When a hardcover is planned, fill in `bindings.hardcover` for KDP and IngramSpark from their templates. Then run a `--proof` build and compare the wrap cover with the template.
