# OI-0006 — Review of the Book 1 Index Term List

## Status

Open

## Date Opened

2026-10-01

## Area

Publishing

## Question

Does the Book 1 index term list (`docs/30-books/31-book-01/index/index-terms.toml`)
cover the headings readers will actually look up, with the right names and
cross-references, and when should the anchor-format index be wired into an
EPUB build?

## Why This Matters

The index mechanism (ADR-03-0007) is in place and every term matches the
manuscript. But choosing what to index, and what to call it, is an editorial
judgement. The first term list was drafted from a full read of all fourteen
chapters and the epilogue. It has 299 headings and subentries, 136
cross-references and about 2,150 locators, and it has not yet had the author's
review.

## Current Assumptions

- Headings are concept-led and named the way a beginner would look them up
  ("Hallucination", "Jobs and AI", "Scams"), with product, organisation and
  study names where the text discusses them.
- Australian institutions are indexed by their common short names (ASIC, OAIC,
  eSafety Commissioner, Scamwatch, TAFE, VET).
- People are indexed only where the prose names them (for example Acemoglu,
  Douglas Adams). Names that appear only in Chapter Notes are not indexed.
- Very broad concepts (generative AI, judgement, prediction, verification,
  prompts and others) index their first mention in each chapter only.
- Privacy (49 locators) and Scams (47) are deliberately left broad because they
  are central topics in Chapters 9 and 13. The line index flags both.

## Points for Review

1. Missing headings the author would expect, or headings that are not useful.
2. Naming: for example "Jobs and AI" versus "Employment", and "Self-driving
   vehicles" versus "Autonomous vehicles".
3. Whether Privacy and Scams should be split into more subentries.
4. Whether the "see also" lines are too generous.
5. Whether a professional indexer should review the list before the first print
   edition.

## Options

### Option 1 — Author reviews the line index and edits the term list

Pros:

- Quick; edits are plain-language phrases, and `report` shows the effect at once.

Cons:

- The author's time.

### Option 2 — Professional indexer review before print

Pros:

- Highest editorial quality for the print edition.

Cons:

- Cost; most useful once the text is close to final.

## Recommended Next Step

Read `docs/30-books/31-book-01/index/book-01-index-lines.md` and the index pages
of a full PDF build, and note changes against the points above. EPUB wiring
should be done when the EPUB build is created: pass `--format anchors` to
`annotate` and `backmatter`.
