# OI-0004 — Remaining National Library Catalogue Metadata for Book 1

## Status

Resolved — 2026-10-04

## Date Opened

2026-09-24

## Area

Publishing

## Question

Which values still need to be settled before Book 1's National Library of Australia prepublication and legal deposit details are submitted?

## Why This Matters

ADR-03-0006 sets the publisher, series, and title hierarchy, but some fields couldn't be completed at first. The same values also feed the ISBN record, the copyright page, the PDF metadata, the website, and the covers, so they should be settled once and used everywhere.

## Current Assumptions

- Publisher: `RePass Cloud Pty Ltd`. Imprint: none (How-To-Use-AI.com is the series, not an imprint; ADR-03-0006, 2026-10-02 amendment). Series number: 1. Edition: first. Format: eBook — PDF. Year: 2026. Audience: General. Genre: Non-fiction (all per ADR-03-0006).

## Items to Settle

1. **Final Book 1 title.**
2. **Exact series title wording.**
3. **ISBN.** One per format, bought under `RePass Cloud Pty Ltd`.
4. **Estimated retail price.**
5. **Expected publication month.**
6. **Creator life dates.**
7. **Copyright holder.**
8. **Publisher category.**

## Answers

| Item | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Title | **AI for Normal People**, subtitle *Understanding Artificial Intelligence Without the Hype*. Confirmed by the author 2026-10-03 (Book 1 OI-0002). |
| 2. Series title | **How-To-Use-AI.com**, hyphenated, used everywhere (books, covers, website, legal copy, docs). Confirmed by the author 2026-10-04. "How To Use AI.com" and "How To Use AI.com Book Series" are retired. |
| 3. ISBN | Bought under RePass Cloud Pty Ltd: paperback 978-1-7649948-0-4, PDF 978-1-7649948-1-1, EPUB 978-1-7649948-2-8 (`publishing/books.json`). |

| 8. Publisher category | Already decided in ADR-03-0006: pick the Company/organisation option (never "Individual / Self"). No author input was needed. |
