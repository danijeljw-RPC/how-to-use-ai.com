# OI-0009 — Colour Paperback Edition: ISBN and Paper

## Status

Open

## Date Opened

2026-10-04

## Area

Publishing

## Question

The author wants release builds to produce a colour and a black-and-white paperback interior for KDP and IngramSpark. What ISBN and paper should the colour edition use?

## Why This Matters

KDP and IngramSpark both set the ink and paper when a paperback title is created, and a title's ink can't be changed after it's published. A colour paperback is therefore a second paperback product with its own title record, and the ISBN agencies treat a different product as needing its own ISBN. The barcode on each cover must match the ISBN of the file it's uploaded with.

The paper also sets the spine width. Colour stock has a different thickness from black-and-white white stock, so the colour edition needs its own covers.

## Items to Settle

1. **Colour ISBN.** Buy a fourth ISBN for the colour paperback (*recommended* if both editions will be sold), or use the colour interior only for private or proof copies with no ISBN on its cover?
2. **KDP colour paper.** Standard colour or premium colour? Premium colour has richer colour and costs more per copy.
3. **IngramSpark colour paper.** Standard colour (50 or 70 lb) or premium colour (70 lb)?
4. **Calipers.** Check the colour-paper thickness for each choice against each printer's cover template, then record it in `books.json`.

## Current Assumptions

- A fourth ISBN will be added as `books[].editions.paperbackColour.isbn`. Proof builds warn if it is missing; real release builds stop.
- KDP premium colour, IngramSpark premium colour 70 lb, with calipers marked "verify against printer template" until checked.
- The black-and-white edition keeps the existing paperback ISBN `978-1-7649948-0-4`.
