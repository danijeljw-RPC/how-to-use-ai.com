# OI-0009 — Colour Paperback Edition: ISBN and Paper

## Status

Open (decisions made 2026-10-04; waiting on the colour ISBN and an IngramSpark caliper check)

## Date Opened

2026-10-04

## Area

Publishing

## Question

The author wants release builds to produce a colour and a black-and-white paperback interior for KDP and IngramSpark. What ISBN and paper should the colour edition use?

## Why This Matters

KDP and IngramSpark both set the ink and paper when a paperback title is created, and a title's ink can't be changed after it's published. A colour paperback is therefore a second paperback product with its own title record, and the ISBN agencies treat a different product as needing its own ISBN. The barcode on each cover must match the ISBN of the file it's uploaded with.

The paper also sets the spine width. Colour stock can be a different thickness from black-and-white white stock, so the colour edition has its own covers.

## Answers (2026-10-04)

| Item | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Colour ISBN | **A separate ISBN**, supplied by the author. The slot is `books[].editions.paperbackColour` in `publishing/books.json` (`isbn`, `isbnDisplay`, `priceCode`, `price`). |
| 2. KDP colour paper | **Standard colour.** KDP's published spine formula is page count × 0.002252 in, the same as white black-and-white paper. |
| 3. IngramSpark colour paper | **Standard colour 50 lb (74 gsm).** The author leaned towards it, and it suits this book: the colour is mostly accents (callout labels, links, diagrams) on text pages, and 50 lb is the cheaper stock. The one risk is the solid navy chapter-opener bands, which can show through or ripple slightly on 50 lb; check them in a printed proof, and move to 70 lb if they bother you. |

Recorded in ADR-03-0010.

## Still Open

1. **Colour ISBN value.** Fill in `editions.paperbackColour.isbn` and `isbnDisplay` (hyphenated, as Thorpe-Bowker shows it). Until then, proof builds warn and name the colour files `31-book-01-paperback-colour_*`; a real release stops.
2. **IngramSpark colour caliper.** `colourPaperCaliperInches` for IngramSpark is set to 0.0025 in per page as a starting value. IngramSpark's help site couldn't be read during this session. Generate an IngramSpark cover template for the final page count with standard colour 50 lb, and if its spine differs, set `colourPaperCaliperInches` or `colourSpineWidthOverrideInches`.
3. **Trim availability.** Confirm 7.5 × 9.25 in is offered with standard colour 50 lb at IngramSpark when setting up the title.
