# OI-0001 — Cross-Referencing Book 1's Four-Part Structure Against the 5-Book Series Arc

## Status

Open

## Date Opened

2026-09-21

## Area

Series

## Question

Should Book 1's four-part structure (Parts 1–4 plus epilogue, per ADR-02-0001) be formally cross-referenced against the 5-book series arc in `docs/01-series/series-structure.md`, so later books can cite which Book 1 parts/chapters they assume as prerequisite knowledge?

## Why This Matters

`docs/01-series/series-structure.md`'s Continuity Rules already require later books to "explicitly reference which earlier-book concepts they assume." Without a formal cross-reference (e.g., a table mapping series-level themes to Book 1 chapters), later books risk re-explaining Book 1 foundations or silently assuming them.

## Current Assumptions

- No formal cross-reference exists yet — this is deferred until Book 2 planning begins, since Book 1 chapter content doesn't exist yet either.
- The working assumption is that this becomes a `docs/01-series/` planning artifact once Book 1 drafting is further along, not before.

## Options

### Option 1 — Defer until Book 1 chapters are drafted

Pros:

- Avoids speculative cross-referencing against content that doesn't exist yet.
- Matches the current linear priority order in `prompt.md` (finish Book 1 chapter-by-chapter first).

Cons:

- Risk of forgetting to do it later.

### Option 2 — Build the cross-reference table now, chapter-title-only

Pros:

- Gets the scaffolding in place early.

Cons:

- Titles/structure could still change before drafting; premature cross-referencing could need rework.

## Recommended Next Step

Defer (Option 1). Revisit this OI when Book 2 planning starts, or once several Book 1 chapters are drafted and their actual content (not just titles) is stable enough to reference.
